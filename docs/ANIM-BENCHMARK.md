# Snabits move animations: the standard

Every move animation is graded against the ones Gid approved between 16 and 17 Nov 2026: Vampire's Wing Wrap, Tome's Slam Shut, Dango's Big Roll, Crease's Paper Boomerang, Bloop's Big Swat, Dagon's Tidal Grasp and Fisher's Song, Shub's Kid Stampede, Shoggoth's Swallow Whole and Yog's Gate Open. Each rule below comes from something he corrected on the way there, and the correction is quoted after it.

## The rules

1. **It looks like its name.** A player who reads the move's name should recognise the animation without the label. "Moss smear doesn't look like it's smearing something." "Big swat doesn't look like a swat."
2. **It uses the Snabit's own body.** A swat swings the Snabit's own tentacles, a wrap uses its own cape, a slam uses its own covers, a roll is the body itself rolling, a bite comes from its own mouth. A prop only appears when the move is about a prop. "Why not use the tentacles to swat instead." "How about we use his actual cape."
3. **The body moves the way the body is built.** Dango is a long roll and rolls end-on to the camera like a rolling pin. A cape hangs vertical from the shoulders. Kitsune's tails rise behind its back. "It should be long body in line with the camera and roll sideways." "Cape should be vertical not horizontal."
4. **The element fits the family.** Lava has no water moves. A cold scoop presses frost on instead of launching a wave. "Why does lava have a water ability?"
5. **Things follow real physics.** Water falls and never floats or climbs with a body. A wave's top is thrown forward and falls in an arc, and the wave is one solid body with no hollow. Anything that stands on the floor stays on the floor when a target is lifted or knocked back. "Does water do that???" "Why does the gate move?"
6. **Curves are smooth.** No sharp corners where two curves meet, and no fast snaps where a slow motion was meant. "Why does the wave have an elbow angled bend?"
7. **Layers make sense.** Something that comes from behind the target is drawn behind it until it wraps round the front. "Appears behind. Not in front."
8. **Timing reads.** A wrap or a grasp closes over about a second, like a one-armed shoulder hug. A move that tells a story after the hit, like a swallow and a spit, holds its turn until the story ends. "It's a wrap not a slap." "Give more time inside the gate."
9. **It's big enough to read on a phone.** An effect that matters is at least the size of the target. "Bigger paper boomerang." "Bigger airwaves."
10. **Props take the attacker's colours.** A thrown paper boomerang is Crease's colour, and Hastur's curtains are Hastur's colour. "Paper boomerang same color as crease." "Change the curtain color same as him."
11. **It has detail.** Shading, a highlight, an outline and texture, the same as the sprites: curtain folds, cloth creases and torn threads, a goat kid with horn nubs and a goat's eye. Flat sticks and plain blobs fail. "Improve quality." "Smoother and more details."
12. **It matches the chibi style.** Anything with a face is drawn cute and round like the Snabits. Shub's kids were redrawn chibi.

## Grades

- **Pass:** meets every rule above.
- **Fix:** right idea with a rule broken, such as too small, too fast, flat, or the wrong colour.
- **Wrong:** the animation shows a different action from the name, or uses a part the Snabit doesn't have.
