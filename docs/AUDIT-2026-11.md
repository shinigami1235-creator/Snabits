# Snabits audit, November 2026

Claude played the game in a headless browser on 14 and 15 November 2026, fixed 14 bugs and ran the balance numbers through the game's own functions. No balance value was changed since nothing measured was far enough out to call broken. Section 4 lists what needs your decision, and section 5 lists what could not be tested from here.

The commits run from `f39d32f` to the commit that adds this file, on top of `e0be061`.

## 3. Balance

No value was changed, so there is no before and after. `balance.py` plays whole lives with `runDrill`, `runRest`, `endWeek`, `startCup`, `finishCup`, `wildFight` and `giveExp`, with the waits made instant and each fight worked out by `arSim`.

### One plan, 39 lives

The plan is 3 lives for each of the 13 ordinary families. Each week it pets and plays, fights up to 3 wild battles while fatigue is under 40 (none on a cup week), enters the monthly cup of its grade and the official cup when Tala's odds are 40% or better, rests at 50 fatigue or 35 stress or the week before the official cup, and otherwise drills one of the family's best stats. It praises a great, scolds a slack and consoles a fail.

| | Result |
|---|---|
| Lifespan | 160 to 184 weeks, median 172 |
| Reached D | 39 of 39, median week 36 |
| Reached C | 39 of 39, median week 60 |
| Reached B | 39 of 39, median week 96 |
| Reached A | 34 of 39, median week 114 |
| Reached S | 24 of 39, median week 132 |
| Level at the end | median 50 |
| Earned points at the end | median 315 |
| Weeks by kind | 62 drills (3 heavy), 51 rests, 40 cups |
| Wild fights | 99 |
| Drill results | 8 great, 9 fail, 6 slack |
| Injuries, colds, weeks lost to strain | 0 in every life |

Grade S is reachable in a normal lifespan. The fastest possible is week 59, since the official cups come every 12 weeks and it takes 5 of them. With no wild fights at all, 12 of 26 lives reached S at a median of week 150. Entering cups only at 60% odds gave 15 of 26 at week 144.

## 4. Proposals that need your OK

1. **Abilities in the arena.** The arena handles 11 abilities: Sky High, Bloodsuck, Rebirth, Illusion, Coil, Thick Fur, Steady, Counterpunch, Volatile, Steeping and Bright. The first eight families' abilities (Wobble, Heavy Lifter, Big Bite, Keen Eye, Shell, Long Stride, Web, Slippery) and all 24 element abilities only work in the old turn battle. This is the known gap and nothing was built.
2. **Specials at low level.** `AR_FAM_DMG` was tuned at level 30. At level 10 five of the six specials win 59% to 65%, since they start with 320 base stat points against 300. A multiplier that changes with level would bring them into the band at every grade, or they can stay strong early as a reward for finding one.
3. **How soon grade S comes.** The comment in `hatch` says a well-raised Snabit reaches S around the middle of its life. The plan above got there at week 132 of 172, and 15 of 39 lives never did. The Official B Cup is the slowest step at 34 wins in 107 tries. Options: hold the official cups every 8 weeks, lower the Official B field, or leave it.
4. **Tala's cup advice.** She says "strong enough for it, so I'd enter" at 60% odds of winning one match, which wins a 6 monster round robin about 1 time in 3. Her advice could use the chance of finishing first.
5. **Fight style on the record page.** The line reads the old turn battle's habits, which arena fights never record, so it always says the Snabit hasn't fought enough. It could be built from the arena habits.
6. **Special abilities through fusion.** A child in an ordinary family can still inherit Rebirth or Sky High from a special parent. Say whether that should stay.
7. **Costumes through fusion.** A fused child never has a costume, though it can inherit a costume skill from its first parent. Say whether the costume should pass on.
8. **Skip scenes and Show hidden numbers.** Both reset on every reload since they live on the ranch state. They could be saved with the player.
9. **Menus during a walk to the shop, the lab or the freezer.** Tapping another menu in the second it takes to walk there lets the first panel open on top afterwards. A guard on the panel would stop it.
10. **Acid Splash.** Fizz's level 20 family move has the same name as the Venom skill, so being hit by it can teach a Venom Snabit the skill and its uses count toward mastery. Renaming the family move would separate them.

## 5. Not tested

- The camera, lens switching, the flashlight and the install prompt, since the browser here has no camera.
- Friend battles in live rooms, since PeerJS needs a server this machine cannot reach.
- The live GitHub Pages site, since github.io is blocked here. The Publish workflow reported success for the first push.
- Fights played by hand with any skill. The drivers start a fight by hand, pick a preset and then switch to Auto.
- Rubbing, petting and barn taps on the ranch canvas, which use real touch gestures.
- How the sounds and the art look and sound to a person. The drivers confirm that every sound plays without an error and every screen draws without one.
- Speed on a real phone, and iOS Safari.
- Hunger across real days. Meals follow the phone's calendar, and one run happened to cross midnight.
- Other plans of play. The balance numbers come from one plan and two variants, with no skill trips, missions, contests or bought items.

Tell Claude which of the 10 proposals to build.

## 6. What Gid picked and what was built (15 Nov 2026)

1. **Abilities in the arena: built.** All 37 family and element abilities fire there now, with a label, colour and sound. Sunbather, Rain Dancer, Night-born, Undertow, Beguile, Long Stride and Halo got arena versions since the arena has no fields or switching, and their descriptions say so. Held items work in the arena too, and Heavy Lifter makes them 50% stronger in both battles. Last Stand went from a 30% to a 20% heal, and Hothead and Toxic Touch from a 20% to a 10% chance, since each added 7 to 12 points of win rate. Now none adds more than about 7 (`ability_balance.py`), every family sits between 46% and 53% (`arena_balance.py`), and `ability_events.py` checks that each one fires.
2. **Specials at low level: left as a reward.**
3. **The climb to S: made easier.** The Official B Cup is a round robin of 4, its field comes from the middle of grade B, and Rook brings a greener Snabit to it. Over 39 lives the cup was won 36 of 70 times, up from 34 of 107. Grade A came at a median of week 108 (was 114) and grade S at week 120 (was 132), with 23 of 39 lives reaching S.
4. **Tala's advice: uses the whole cup.** `cupChances` turns her sample of single matches into the chance of first place for the cup's format, and she says the number. `cupOdds` still gives the single-match share for the sims.
5. **Fight style: built from the arena picks.** The record page reads the moves you picked in arena fights, the range it likes, and falls back to the old habits.
6. **Special abilities through fusion: locked to the special families.**
7. **Costumes through fusion: pass on at a lower rate,** 30% from the first parent and 15% from the second, with the costume's two skills.
8. **Skip scenes and Show hidden numbers: saved with the player.**
9. **Menus during a walk: fixed** with a ticket each panel takes before the walk (`visitTicket` in `npcs.js`).
10. **Acid Splash: Fizz's move is now Beaker Splash.**
