# Snabits art direction: handout

This is the handout for the Snabits art work. The pixel monsters were replaced with smooth chibi monsters drawn the way Nong Tape was drawn for the Apex Slim site, with the Mega drawn first and detail taken away for Byte and Bit. The new art is built, pushed and the default since 12 Nov 2026. `#art=pixel` at the end of the address brings back the pixel game. Read "Start here" first.

## Start here

Snabits is Gid's Monster Rancher 2-style phone web game where a camera photo becomes a monster you raise, train and fight. As of 14 Nov 2026 every fight in the smooth art runs on the new real-time arena (`arena.js`, `arena-ui.js`, `arena-fx.js`), with statuses, support skills, dodges and its own physical animations, and Gid is playing it and sending notes. The game is live at the address below and every push to main publishes a new build, so a change is on his phone a minute or two after it's pushed. Read Decided for what's approved, Next for what's open and How Gid works before doing anything.

Before writing any line a player reads, load the my-writing-style and humanizer skills, then run the string lint before every push:

    node tools/lint-strings.mjs          checks the game and the README, fails on an error
    node tools/lint-strings.mjs --test   checks the rules still catch what Gid rejected

Gid caught AI slop in the storyboard lines on 12 Nov 2026 because the skills weren't loaded first, and he asked for the lint so it can't happen again. It fails on dashes, semicolons, curly quotes, "That is" glosses, "not X but Y", split contrasts, beats like "Mostly.", reassurance lines, fragment openers like "Four weeks, and ..." and the banned words. It warns on short verbless sentences and mid-line colons, which need a person to read them. A line that breaks a rule on purpose goes in `ALLOW` at the top of the file with the reason.

The tests are in `tools/tests` (see Where everything is). Run the regression set after any change to the arena or the drawing, and show Gid stills or a short clip of anything he'll see before calling it done.

## Decided

- **Smooth monsters over pixel art.** The backgrounds get redrawn flat to match, and Gid is fine redrawing everything.
- **Element:** colour at every stage, small element marks on the sides of the face, and a body pattern at Mega (stripes, spots and so on). Nothing from the element goes on the head, since the head holds the modular parts.
- **Chomp** was approved first: a squat box with small teeth at Bit, tiger stripes as the Brawl pattern.
- **Every family looks clearly different.** Pinch is a crab shell. Antik is an old TV with its face on the screen.
- **Booly's trait is a wisp:** a blue ghost flame circling its head, so it reads as more than a plain ghost. Yurei (a forehead triangle and a misty bottom) and Sheet (bedsheet folds and a patch) were tried and dropped.
- **The five new families (14 Nov 2026)** are in the game and as common as the first eight, so the bestiary has 13 families and 156 kinds. They come from photos whose main colour sits gathered in one spot (`pickFamily` in `monster.js`, the `solid` split), and in a 3,000-photo test Plod was the rarest at about 1 in 65.
  - **Plod** replaced Quad. Gid asked for Chomp's shape with a new look and picked trunk and ears (concept B): flapping ears, a swinging trunk, four stumpy legs and tusks at Mega. The tank, with the ability Steady (pushes on it are halved) and the signature Trunk Slam.
  - **Bipped** is the boxer, with Counterpunch (after a dodge its next Strike hits 20% harder) and One-Two.
  - **Fizz** is the old Flask, renamed by Gid. It throws potions, with Volatile (its statuses last 30% longer) and Potion Toss, whose status follows its element (`POTION_BY_EL` in `skills.js`).
  - **Teep** is the teapot and the steady caster, with Steeping (focus fills 10% faster while it stands still) and Steam Jet.
  - **Lern** is the paper lantern and the quick caster, with Bright (Blind can't land on it and its hit chance can't be lowered) and Flash.
  - Each has 8 arena moves (`FAM_MOVES`), a walk speed, a summon entrance (`ENTER` in `summon.js`), a cry and a pixel body. Gid OK'd the entrance and signature stills. Bipped's entrance throws two jabs, since a wave hid its stub arms behind its body.
- **Special Snabits and costumes (14 Nov 2026)** come from colour easter eggs: a photo with the right mix of colours in the right places (`eggOf` in `monster.js`, fed by `raw` and `g12` from `scan()` in `index.html`, which give each colour's share and where it sits on a 12 by 12 grid). Gid approved the concept sheets (`specials-concept.png`, `costumes-concept.png`) and asked for the Phoenix to look less like an egg, so it's now a plump firebird with a flame crest, flame wings and tail plumes with golden eye-spots.
  - The six specials, each with its own ability, signature move at level 5 with its own animation, eight arena moves, entrance and cry: Eagle (US flag: red and white stripes with a blue corner; Sky High, the first hit it lands is a critical; Talon Dive), Vampire (mostly black, some white, a little red; Bloodsuck, Strikes heal 15% of the damage; Night Bite), Phoenix (red low, orange, yellow high; Rebirth, rises once with a quarter of its HP; Sun Flare), Kitsune (Japan flag: white with a red dot in the centre; Illusion, the first attack hits a fox-fire copy; Foxfire), Naga (a green band with yellow; Coil, a Close Strike on it may Root the attacker; Venom Coil), Yeti (snow white and silver with a little ice blue; Thick Fur, Blaze and Frost hit 25% softer and it can't be Frozen; Avalanche).
  - Specials always have their family ability, keep the element the photo gave them, and only fusion changes their element (Gid's call, since the colours are what summoned them). Parts, marks, worn items and types work on them like any Snabit. They never turn up as random opponents.
  - Costumes paint over whatever family the photo makes and recolour its arms and legs: Panda (black and white near half and half), Bee (yellow and black stripes), Ladybug (red with black spots), Watermelon (green around red with black seeds), Candy cane (red and white stripes). Each costume brings one skill at once and teaches a second at level 6 (`COSTUME_SKILLS` in `skills.js`): Bamboo Smack and Roly-Poly, Stinger and Swarm, Shell Bash and Lucky Spots, Seed Spit and Melon Smash, Cane Hook and Sugar Rush.
  - The scan card says "Special" or "Panda costume", and the bestiary has a Secrets tab with a hint for each one not found yet.
  - Tested on drawn flags and patterns: all 11 trigger, plain photos and the mug scene don't, and about 4% of random cluttered pictures hit one. Real photos still need a try on Gid's phone.
- **The wood theme and icons (14 Nov 2026),** approved from `ui-wood-compare.png` and `icons-sheet.png`, are on by default (`theme.css`, `icons.js`, `body.theme-wood`). Barn wood behind everything, parchment panels with brown ink, buttons on a darker edge 4px below them (the monsters' depth rule), Fredoka as the round font, gold kept for the main button and whatever is selected. The coloured stripe down the side of choice buttons is gone. Icons are one set in the monsters' style, put on the ranch menu, top bar and fight controls by `iconize()`, which reruns whenever the page changes.
- **Sounds everywhere (14 Nov 2026),** at Gid's request: every arena move kind has its own start or release sound (`KSND` in `chibi-hook.js`, timed to the release after a charge), specials get a rising swell, abilities each have one (`atSfx("ab:...")` in `arena-fx.js`), and so do cleanse, thaw, endure, recoil, focus steal, sleep, wake, steps and a knockout. On the ranch, trips and drills, the particles and results sound too (`rsfx` in `ranch.js`): hearts, sparkles, dust, the light bulb, drips, hops, and a stinger for Great, Success, Fail and Slacked off. Each new family and special has a cry.
- **Every ability works in the arena (15 Nov 2026),** after the Fable audit and Gid's picks from its proposals (`ab()`, `hm()`, `slips()` and `undertow()` in `arena.js`, the labels and sounds in `AT_ABIL` and `atSfx` in `arena-fx.js`). The first eight families' abilities and all 24 element abilities now fire there, each with its name, colour and sound. Abilities built around fields or switching got arena versions: Sunbather heals 1% every 4 seconds, Rain Dancer is +15% Speed, Night-born is +10% Mind, Undertow slows the opponent backing away while it's above half HP, Beguile and Long Stride work at the start of the fight, and Halo heals when it braces. Held items work in the arena too (the Element stone, Energy cell, Life seed and Lucky bit), and Heavy Lifter makes them 50% stronger in both battles. Each ability adds at most about 7 points of win rate (`tools/tests/audit/ability_balance.py`), after Last Stand dropped to a 20% heal and Hothead and Toxic Touch to a 10% chance. Passive ones show their label once, the first time they matter in a fight.
- **Fusion (15 Nov 2026):** the six specials' abilities stay with the special families, and a costume passes on 30% of the time from the first parent and 15% from the second, bringing its two skills (`fuseChild` in `lab.js`).
- **The climb to S (15 Nov 2026):** the Official B Cup is a round robin of 4 instead of 6, its field comes from the middle of grade B, and Rook brings a greener Snabit to it. Over 39 simulated lives it was won 36 of 70 times (34 of 107 before), and grade S came at a median of week 120 instead of 132. Josie now gives the chance of taking first place, worked out from the cup's format, as well as whether to enter (`cupChances` in `tourney.js`).
- **Drill music (15 Nov 2026):** each drill has its own short tune, Drill Time, in D at 140 with a snare count-in, and the result cuts it off with a sting: a fanfare for Great, three notes up for Success, a wah-wah for Fail and a lazy slide for Slacked off (`TRACKS.drill` and `musSting` in `music.js`). It's quiet after the sting until the next morning's tune. Every change of tune crossfades, and the drill and the stings swap faster so they land with the screen.
- **Cups the MR2 way (15 Nov 2026),** approved from `cup-screens-concept.png`. Cups no longer goes to the stadium lobby: the month opens under the ranch as a grid of grade tiles (weeks across, grades S to E plus Other and Events down the side), and the picked cup's week, grade, format, entries and prize show in a strip over the ranch (`openCupCal` in `cupcal.js`, styles at the end of `theme.css`). A round robin opens with its table, numbered entrants down the side and a red circle for each win, a blue cross for each loss and a yellow triangle for a draw, and the table comes back after every round with Mira saying who leads (`B.table`, `tableLine`). A knockout opens with the draw, entrants numbered down the side and lines joining pairs to the trophy, and it comes back after every round with the winners' lines in gold (`B.draw8`, `drawLine`). The stadium lobby (`venueLobby`) is kept for the test drivers.
- **Recorded sounds under the synth (15 Nov 2026),** from the CC0 files bundled with the sound-effects skill (`prototype/sfx/`, credits in `sfx/CREDITS.txt`, `smp()` in `chibi-hook.js`): real impacts under light, medium and heavy hits, a deep boom under a knockout, whooshes under moves that swing, and a soft click on buttons. Each play picks one of the takes and nudges its pitch, and all of it follows the music switch.
- **Lullaby (15 Nov 2026):** "Hay Bed", a music-box tune in F at 80, plays once it lies down to sleep on a rest week, in the hut, or after a long day away. It fades out as the morning comes up (`TRACKS.lull` in `music.js`, `RS.lull` in `ranch.js`).
- **Drill props and reactions (15 Nov 2026):** the boulder, rock, puzzle box, books, log and hurdles are in place before the scene fades in (`DRILL_PROPS` in `ranch.js`). Brace takes each ball and slides back, with a heavy third ball. Dodgeball has low balls to jump and high balls to duck flat under. The Fortress log hits at chest height at the bottom of its swing, and Library leans in to read each book.
- **Great drills (15 Nov 2026):** every great does something a success can't, then ends in the same gold burst with confetti (`greatBurst` in `ranch.js`). It throws the boulder overhead, breaks the pitching machine, catches and fires back the last dodgeball, swims five lengths faster each time, sprints the last lap, sprints through the marathon tape, splits the rock so the halves tip over, tears the Fortress log off its rope and breaks it, brings the library books flying to it, parts the waterfall, and clears the last two hurdles in one jump.
- **Swimming (15 Nov 2026):** only the top half shows above the water. Wake rings spread behind it, and at speed it throws spray and speed lines.
- **Drill sounds (15 Nov 2026):** synth sounds and the recorded impacts in `rsfx` (`ranch.js`): footsteps on running drills, swim strokes on a beat that quickens with speed, big splashes at the wall and the dive, the ball machine's thunk, ball hits, a rumbling boulder, the machine rattling, sparking and hissing, stone cracking, the log creaking, the rope snapping and the wood splitting, page flips and flapping books, the waterfall rushing, and a popper with the confetti.
- **Expeditions (15 Nov 2026, `explore.js`):** Out, then Expeditions. Walt has a League permit for each open region, and the Rift's edge opens at grade C. An expedition takes the week and each region can be explored once a season. The map under the speech box is circles joined by paths with fog over what hasn't been reached. Finds are chests, springs, wild Snabits with a League bounty, a page of Helena's journal and a Pattern Stone at the far end, which lets the Snaboscope find that special. Until then a scan that would make one makes one of the first eight, and the bestiary marks them Locked with how each opens. Licence Cups run in week 4 of the first two months of each season except Summer, with a field of that family only, and grow with the grade: 4 entrants at E and D, 6 at C and B, a knockout of 8 at A and S (`LICENCE`, `UNLOCK_HOW`, `famOpen` in `game.js`, `licenceSize` in `tourney.js`). The story behind it is in `STORY/WORLD.md`.
- **Names (15 Nov 2026):** Tala is now Josie, Kiko is Walt and Nena is Nora, since Gid wants names that read well anywhere. Code ids like `tala.js`, `'tala'`, `'guide'` and `'shop'` stayed the same.
- **Twelve costumes (15 Nov 2026),** approved from `costumes2-concept.png`: Tiger, Zebra, Cow, Penguin, Strawberry, Rubber duck and Clownfish joined the first five, each drawn in `costumeBehind` and `costumeOn` in `chibi.js` with two skills in `COSTUME_SKILLS`. Each costume opens its own way, and the game never says how (Gid's call, for surprise): contests (Bee, Zebra, Penguin), festivals (Strawberry, Watermelon, Candy cane), good trip weeks (Tiger, Clownfish, Ladybug), the bestiary (Panda at 30 seen, Cow at 10 raised) and a Duck pattern card in Nora's shop. Before then the pattern makes a plain Snabit and its Secrets hint shows ???. The bestiary no longer says how a locked family opens either. `tools/tests/audit/eggs.py` draws all 18 patterns.
- **Your own monster is seen from behind in battle,** and the opponent faces you. Beams and eye or mouth attacks from behind start just past the body's edge on the enemy's side.
- **Hits show an ouch face:** squeezed eyes and gritted teeth.
- **Flashes are for big hits only.** The white body flash, the dark impact frame, the crowd cheer and the lightning screen flash only happen on crits and super-effective hits. Ordinary hits get a short freeze, a small shake and a slight brighten.
- **Effects grow with a skill's power.** Under 55 total power a skill draws small, 55 to 94 medium, 95 and up big, with more pieces and a bigger blast (`movePower` in `moves.js`). Gid asked for weaker moves to look weaker and stronger versions to get the better effects.
- **Support skills have their own effects:** arrows up for buffs, arrows down for drops, a hex bubble for shields (ice for Frost Armor, rock for Shard Wall, steel for Iron Wall), sparkles for heals, a gathering glow on charge turns, and a screen-wide change for fields (sun rays, rain, snow, static, moonlight).
- **Signature skills (11 Nov 2026).** Each family learns its own move at level 5, and worn wings teach Fly. They never appear in scan pools, shops or trips (`SIG_SKILLS`, `FAMILY_SIG`, `sigSkills()` in `skills.js`):
  - Skit: a web in its own element (Fire Web burns, Ice Web freezes, Water Web soaks, and so on), 40 power, always lowers Speed.
  - Pinch: Bunker Down, raises Constitution 2 and Insight 1, drawn as the crab tucking into its shell.
  - Chomp: Crunch, 80 power, may lower Constitution. Squib: Ink Jet, 40 power, 40% Blind. Antik: Static Screen, 45 power, 30% Daze. Moko: Glare, lowers Power and Speed. Stilt: High Kick, 90 power. Booly: Phase Shift, +30% dodge for 3 turns.
  - Bat wings or Angel wings: Fly, 90 power, goes up out of sight on the first turn and dives on the second.
- **New skills, learnable outside the scan pool:** Fire Spin (Blaze, 25 x 3, Burn), Shadow Strike (Shade, 85, can't be dodged, rises out of the target's shadow), Gust (neutral chip, 40, goes first) and Tornado (neutral chip, 90, may lower Speed). Leaf Storm, Inferno and Maelstrom now play as leaf, fire and water tornadoes.
- **Marks look the same on every family (11 Nov 2026).** Stout makes the body 12% wider with a pale belly. On Booly the belly is a soft glow at the base of the cone, since Gid read a pale oval under its mouth as a beard (14 Nov 2026). Horned puts two horns on the head. Shelled puts a spiked shell on the back, with the rim and spikes showing round the body from the front and the plates from behind. Sage adds two feathery feelers with glowing tips. Haloed floats a gold ring over the head, raised above any hat, horns, feelers or Antik's antenna. Winged adds small feathered wings in the body's colours, and worn wings take their place.
- **Care variants are drawn on every family.** Devoted gets a pink glow and a heart that drifts up. Pampered gets a rounder body, star sparkles in its eyes and a couple of sparkles round it. Feral gets a dark outline, two fangs and narrowed eyes at every stage. Bulky is 12% wider, and Lean is narrower and taller. Wanderer and Moonlit change colours, and Moonlit also gets silver eyes and a few stars. Champion wears a gold medal on a ribbon, with a gold star on its back from behind. Scruffy has tufts of fur round its edge and a few dirt spots.
- **Fusion hybrids (12 Nov 2026)** stand any family's body on any other family's legs, in the smooth art too. A body on Booly's legs floats on a ghost tail, and a body on Squib's legs gets a jelly skirt with tapered tentacles. A hybrid learns its body family's signature skill and stands at the average height of both parents (`hybrid()` in `chibi.js`).
- **Evolution is a surprise.** The scan card no longer shows what the Byte form will look like.
- **The info page (11 Nov 2026)** follows Monster Rancher 2's monster data, with three tabs. It opens from the Info button in the ranch's top bar, by tapping the name, from Care > Skills, and from the skill book's Back button.
  - Status: a portrait, the stage, level, grade, age, height and build, loyalty hearts, the six stats with family ranks, personality, ability, likes and dislikes, a Spoiled to Strict bar for how it's been raised, fatigue and stress as five-step meters, its condition (including "Hungry, no meal yet today"), its marks and variant, and a line from Josie.
  - Skills: four slots and the full list of learned skills, with filters for Strike, Cast and Support. Pick a slot, then tap a skill to put it there. A skill already in another slot swaps over.
  - Record: wins, losses, cups won, fight style, favourite move, mastered skills, trips, marks, birthday, and what it did each of the last 8 weeks.
- **Every stage fights with 4 skills,** picked from everything it has learned. Evolution keeps its stat bonus and new look, and it no longer opens slots. There's one loadout and no saved sets. Computer opponents also get 4.
- **Likes and dislikes, the MR2 way.** Each Snabit has one like and one dislike. Either can be a meal (30%), a drill (30%), a snack (15%), something to wear (10%) or battles (15%). Both read "Not found yet" on the info page until they happen the first time, and Josie says so when they do (`TASTE_KINDS`, `taste()` in `ranch.js`).
  - Meal: the old meal effects.
  - Drill: −4 stress and +2 spoil if liked, +8 stress and −2 spoil if disliked.
  - Snack: −5 stress and +3 spoil if liked, +8 stress and −3 spoil if disliked.
  - Something to wear: −3 stress a week if liked, +4 if disliked.
  - Battles: −4 stress and +2 spoil after a fight if liked, +6 stress and −1 spoil if disliked.
  - Monsters from older saves keep their meal like and dislike.
- **Meals follow the phone's calendar (12 Nov 2026),** which sets Snabits apart from MR2's monthly feeding. One meal a day, whatever the game week. Each day it went without one adds 5 stress, 3 weight off and 1 spoil off, up to 4 days, and Josie says so when the ranch opens on a new day (`dayKey()`, `fedToday()`, `hungerCheck()` in `ranch.js`). Snacks stay at one per game week.
- **Item descriptions hint, they don't spell out.** Every meal, snack, item, held item and worn item has a short line like "A minty leaf that relieves stress." or "Sweet and fattening. It'll love you for it." (`DESC` in `game.js`). The exact effect shows after it in brackets when Show hidden numbers is on. The Bitter root's line is "Tastes awful. It listens better afterwards." since in this game it disciplines rather than heals. Gid's own example called it a recovery item, so that can change if he wants it to heal.
- **Training happens at its own place (12 Nov 2026),** MR2-style. Picking a drill fades to black and opens on its place with the drill tune. The result, the stat pops and the praise or scold happen there, then the sun sets, night falls, it goes black, and morning comes up at home (`DRILL_PLACE`, `HEAVY_PLACE`, `goPlace()`, `backHome()` in `ranch.js`). Pixel mode keeps the everyday drills in the yard.

## Construction rules taken from Nong Tape

- **Depth:** a darker copy of the body sits 4px lower and works as both outline and depth. The backgrounds use the same trick.
- **Highlight:** one white arc at 55% opacity on the upper left.
- **Eyes:** big and dark, with one large and one small white sparkle.
- **Cheeks:** pink blush.
- **Limbs:** thick lines with round ends.
- **Colour:** every colour comes from the photo's shades.
- **Motion:** arms and legs are numbers, so any pose blends into any other.

## Limits the art has to live with

- Every monster is generated from a photo, so the art is a parts system that works for any colour and any mix of family, mark, variant and hat.
- It runs in phone browsers, often several monsters at once in the stadium, so drawing stays on canvas at 60 frames a second. The arena background only redraws when its size changes or the crowd moves.
- The players are kids, but there's competitive depth, so the monsters look cute and still look strong at Mega.
